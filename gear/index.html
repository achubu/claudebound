Designer

Pixel-art sets: flat tones, a black outline and neon panel lines, the same palette on every slot.

Make gear without drawing: the slot's fit template is the shape, a texture fills it, his own shading is kept, and trim, bands and a seam are drawn on every frame. The hero above wears it as you change it. Save to game writes a small recipe file (gear/recipes/<item>.json) through GitHub's new-file page: commit it and the game draws the item that way (in place of its drawn look).
