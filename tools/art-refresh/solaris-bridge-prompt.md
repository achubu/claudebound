# Solaris bridge (Round 518)

Owner: "lets just generate an over the top bridge graphic we can use for bridges instead that matches the aesthetic". One
complete prompt. The bridge is modular so it fits every crossing without being stretched (owner rule: never distort art):
a west end, a middle section that repeats seamlessly, an east end. In the game the three pieces are scaled by one factor so the
deck matches the road's width, the middle repeats to span the river, and the whole is turned 90 degrees for roads running north
to south (so it is drawn straight down, with no front face that would look wrong when turned). Row 1 is the whole bridge, for
reference and for short crossings.

```
A sprite sheet of one grand ornate stone bridge for a top-down 2D game, matching a set of cartoon desert town buildings
(sandstone and adobe houses, domes, pyramids, palm oases) drawn in clean, crisp game art with dark outlines. On a flat solid
pure green background (#00FF00), with no gradient, pattern or texture in the background.

THE BRIDGE: an over-the-top, monumental desert bridge for a road crossing a river: a wide paved deck of large sandstone slabs
with a decorative inlaid border of turquoise and terracotta tiles running along both edges; tall carved sandstone
balustrades along both sides with ornate pillars; at each end a pair of grand gate towers with small gold domes, standing on
the corners of the deck; tall lantern posts with bronze lanterns and a few hanging cloth banners in faded red and blue along the
balustrades. The deck runs left to right; its two long sides are the top and bottom edges.

LAYOUT: two rows, with clear green space between every piece and round the edge of the sheet. No labels, numbers, letters or
text anywhere.
ROW 1: the whole bridge, about 4 times as long as it is wide, centred.
ROW 2: the same bridge cut into three separate pieces, side by side with green space between them, all at exactly the same
scale and the same width (top edge to bottom edge) as the bridge in row 1:
  PIECE 1, the WEST END: the left gate towers and the first part of the deck; its right edge is a straight vertical cut through
  the deck.
  PIECE 2, the MIDDLE SECTION: a square length of deck with balustrades, one lantern post on each side in its middle; its left
  and right edges are straight vertical cuts, and it repeats seamlessly: its left edge continues exactly into its right edge,
  so many copies placed end to end make one unbroken bridge.
  PIECE 3, the EAST END: the last part of the deck and the right gate towers; its left edge is a straight vertical cut through
  the deck, matching the middle section exactly.

VIEW: straight down from directly above (orthographic, no perspective, no tilt): you see the deck from above and the tops of
the balustrades, pillars, towers and lanterns. No front faces or side walls are visible, so the bridge looks the same when
turned 90 degrees.

STYLE: detailed 2D game art, crisp clean shapes and thin dark line work, flat colours with light, simple shading, a 1-pixel
dark outline round each piece. A sunny desert town: warm golds, ochres, sandstone, terracotta, turquoise tiles, polished bronze
and gold accents.

NOT IN THE PICTURE: no water, no river, no banks, no ground, no road beyond the bridge; no cast shadows; no glow; no people,
carts or animals; nothing floating between the pieces.
```
