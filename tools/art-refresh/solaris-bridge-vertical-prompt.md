# Solaris bridge, north to south (Round 526)

Owner: "we need a seperate graphic for a vertical bridge". The east-west bridge (Round 519) turned 90 degrees shows its domes and
banners lying on their side. One complete prompt for the same bridge running top to bottom, drawn the way the buildings are (seen
from above, tilted 30 degrees, so the towers stand up), in three pieces like the first sheet: the north end, a middle section that
repeats seamlessly downward, and the south end. In the game the pieces are scaled by one factor so the deck covers the road
(Round 526: 1.3 x its width) and the middle repeats across the river.

```
A sprite sheet of one grand ornate stone bridge for a top-down 2D game, matching a set of cartoon desert town buildings
(sandstone and adobe houses, domes, pyramids, palm oases) drawn in clean, crisp game art with dark outlines. On a flat solid
pure green background (#00FF00), with no gradient, pattern or texture in the background.

THE BRIDGE: an over-the-top, monumental desert bridge for a road crossing a river: a wide paved deck of large sandstone slabs
with a decorative inlaid border of turquoise and terracotta tiles running along both edges; tall carved sandstone balustrades
along both sides with ornate pillars; at each end a pair of grand gate towers with small gold domes, standing on the corners of
the deck; tall lantern posts with bronze lanterns and a few hanging cloth banners in faded red and blue along the balustrades.
THE DECK RUNS FROM THE TOP OF THE IMAGE TO THE BOTTOM: its two long sides, with the balustrades, are its left and right edges.

LAYOUT: three columns, with clear green space between every piece and round the edge of the sheet. No labels, numbers, letters

COLUMN 1: the whole bridge, about 4 times as tall as it is wide, centred.
COLUMNS 2 AND 3: the same bridge cut into three separate pieces, at exactly the same scale and the same width (left edge to
right edge) as the bridge in column 1, one above another in column 2 and column 3 with green space between them:
  PIECE 1 (top of column 2), the NORTH END: the top pair of gate towers and the first part of the deck; its bottom edge is a
  straight horizontal cut through the deck.
  PIECE 2 (bottom of column 2), the MIDDLE SECTION: a square length of deck with balustrades, one lantern post on each side in
```
