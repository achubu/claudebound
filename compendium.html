Boss Encounters

Touch Crystals Boss Encounter

An intense multi-phase boss fight centered around controlling crystal conduits. Players must manipulate and touch energy crystals in sequence to remove the boss's invulnerability shield while managing wave reinforcements.

  • Phase 1: Direct engagement; Boss shields at 75% Health threshold.
  • Phase 2: Crystal Activation; Requires active position management to overload energy nodes.
  • Phase 3: Overdrive Enrage; High AOE damage output until defeated.
