Cardbound — environment art credits

Neon Aftermath city

The active city background atlas was generated for Cardbound using the built-in image-generation tool. Six original district scenes: refuge, shopping district, transit, flooded canals, power plant, and corporate citadel. City scene code adds service bridges, utility barriers, signage, and weather. The previously used LPC atlases below are retained as legacy assets and are not used in the current city renderer.

Meadowrest uses Liberated Pixel Cup artwork. Source atlases are unmodified. The Cardbound scene composition and its art adaptations are offered under CC BY-SA 3.0. This notice applies to these environment assets, not unrelated character artwork.

  • LPC Terrains — bluecarrot16 and contributors listed below.
  • LPC Trees — bluecarrot16 and contributors listed below.
  • LPC Thatched-roof Cottage — bluecarrot16, based on LPC Base Assets by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum), and LPC art entry by Casper Nilsson. CC BY-SA 3.0. Base assets; Casper Nilsson source.
