# Neon Aftermath

All 24 stable room IDs are configured in `neon-city.js`. Existing progression, relic IDs and encounter types are retained. Six district backgrounds are drawn from a 2-column, 3-row original atlas. Each area has its own name, signage, sector marking and deterministic utility-barrier placement. Flooded districts have traversable maintenance bridges. Corner architecture and utility barriers share collision definitions. Legacy positions inside new walls are moved to the central safe road.

Art generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt: six detailed top-down pixel-art scenes in a modern neon city in ruins, arranged in two columns and three rows: survivor refuge, shopping district, subway, flooded canals, power plant, corporate citadel. Preserve broad central horizontal and vertical traversable roads in each scene. Corner blocks contain damaged buildings, cables, debris and neon illumination. No characters or interface elements.

The generated atlas is encoded as WebP for faster browser loading; its composition is unchanged. Runtime canvas sampling selects a district cell. The existing LPC credits cover legacy files only, not the new atlas.
